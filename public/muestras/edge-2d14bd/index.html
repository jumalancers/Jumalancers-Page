Home detailing · Signature

What a deep clean covers

Every item. Every surface.

The One-Time Deep Cleaning checklist, room by room. Anything else, just ask.

Common areas R/01

  • Cobwebs removed
  • Blinds, light fixtures, wall decorations, baseboards
  • Windowsills and stair handrails
  • Mirrors and patio / balcony glass doors
  • Carpets vacuumed, hard floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Beds made, linens changed if provided

Kitchen R/02

  • Cabinets, fridge, oven and dishwasher outside
  • Microwave inside and out
  • Stove top and hood
  • Small appliances wiped
  • Counters, sinks, backsplash, windows
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped

Bathrooms R/03

  • Cabinets outside
  • Toilet inside and out
  • Shower and tub
  • Mirrors, sinks, counters
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Trash taken out

  • Washer and dryer exterior

FAQ

Before you book.

What services do you offer?

Residential cleaning, one-time deep cleaning (home detailing) and commercial cleaning. Also move-in / move-out, special occasion, post-construction, fridge and oven, window cleaning, pressure washing and car detailing.

Are your products safe for pets and children?

Yes. We use eco-friendly products that are safe for pets and children, along with hypoallergenic vacuums.

How do I schedule a cleaning?

Send the form on this page or call (425) 873-8592, Monday to Friday, 8 AM–5 PM. Scheduling is flexible.

How can I pay?

Cash, check and all major credit cards.

Can I customize my cleaning?

Absolutely. Every visit can be customized and special requests are welcome. Tell us what matters most and we'll focus there.
