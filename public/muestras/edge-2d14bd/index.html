FAQ

Before you book.

Still curious? Call (425) 873-8592.

What services do you offer?

Residential cleaning, one-time deep cleaning (home detailing) and commercial cleaning. Also move-in / move-out, special occasion, post-construction, fridge and oven, window cleaning, pressure washing and car detailing.

Are your products safe for pets and children?

Yes. We use eco-friendly products that are safe for pets and children, along with hypoallergenic vacuums.

How do I schedule a cleaning?

Send the form on this page or call (425) 873-8592, Monday to Friday, 8 AM–5 PM. Scheduling is flexible.

How can I pay?

Cash, check and all major credit cards.

Can I customize my cleaning?

Absolutely. Every visit can be customized and special requests are welcome. Tell us what matters most and we'll focus there.
