Everything a deep cleaning includes

Common areas & bedrooms

  • Cobwebs removed
  • Blinds, light fixtures, wall decorations, baseboards
  • Windowsills and stair handrails
  • Mirrors, patio and balcony glass doors
  • Carpets vacuumed, hard floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Beds made, linens changed if provided

Kitchen

  • Cabinets, fridge, oven and dishwasher outside
  • Microwave inside and out
  • Stove top and hood
  • Small appliances
  • Counters, sinks, backsplash, windows
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped

Bathrooms

  • Cabinets outside
  • Toilet inside and out
  • Shower and tub
  • Mirrors, sinks, counters

FAQ

Questions, answered.

From the EDGE website, plus a few Sergio answers in his own videos.

What services does EDGE offer?

A range of cleaning services for homes, including regular cleaning, deep cleaning, move-in/move-out cleaning, special occasion cleaning, fridge cleaning, oven cleaning, window cleaning, pressure washing, car detailing, and more.

What makes EDGE different from other cleaning companies?

EDGE is a family-owned business that takes pride in its work and values your satisfaction. We use advanced techniques and eco-friendly products to ensure your home is thoroughly and safely cleaned, and our team is professionally trained and equipped with the best tools and products.

How do I schedule a cleaning?

Contact us through the website or give us a call at (425) 873-8592. We'll work with you to determine the best cleaning plan for your needs and schedule a convenient time.

Are your products safe for pets and children?

Yes. We use eco-friendly cleaning products that are hypoallergenic and free of harmful chemicals.

How can I pay?

Cash, check, and all major credit cards. We'll discuss payment options with you at the time of booking.

Can I customize my cleaning?

Yes. Every home is unique and has its own set of cleaning needs. Just let us know what you need, and we'll create a cleaning plan that meets your needs and budget.

Should I clean before the cleaner comes?
Sergio answers

“There's one thing you should always do before your cleaner shows up, and it's not cleaning, it's organizing.”

Why can a deep cleaning take so long?
Sergio answers

“Most cleaning companies offer deep cleanings that take eight to 12 hours. Ours can take over 100 hours, not because we're slow, but because we're doing work most companies don't even offer.”

Why does a bathroom take you so long?
Sergio answers

“On average, it takes a cleaner about one hour to clean a bathroom. It takes us eight hours with a team of multiple people.” When he started, he learned: “I was only cleaning the dirt I could see.”

I already have a weekly cleaner. Why a deep clean?
Sergio answers

“Most cleaners only clean what you can see, but all the dirt and grease they leave behind keeps building up for years.”
